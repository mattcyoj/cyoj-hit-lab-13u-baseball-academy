13U Player Development

Development first.
Standards every day.

The CYOJ Hit Lab 13U Baseball Academy is built around developing complete baseball players in an environment that values fundamentals, preparation, confidence, effort and accountability.

At 13U, athletes are entering an important stage of baseball development. Training is designed to reinforce strong mechanics and baseball fundamentals while helping players improve their decision-making, preparation, communication and understanding of team baseball.

Coaching standards within the Academy emphasize purposeful instruction, clear expectations and consistent development. Athletes are expected to compete, learn from mistakes, support teammates and take ownership of their continued growth.
