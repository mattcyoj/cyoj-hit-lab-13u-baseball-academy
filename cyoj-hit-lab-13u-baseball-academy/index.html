Meet the coach

Development first.
Standards every day.

Alex Yeaney serves as a CYOJ Hit Lab baseball coach and athletic development mentor. A former all-state athlete and Western Oregon University baseball player, he brings a player-development mindset to the 14U program.

His coaching centers on strong fundamentals, athletic growth, confidence, preparation and the habits that help players improve on and off the field.
