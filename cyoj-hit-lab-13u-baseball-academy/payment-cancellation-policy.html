2027 13U Baseball Academy

Payment &
Cancellation Policy

This policy explains the financial commitment associated with accepting a 2027 CYOJ Hit Lab 13U Baseball Academy roster spot, including payment obligations, scheduled installments and voluntary withdrawal.

Season Package: $2,500 Commitment: Full Season Program: 2027 13U Baseball Academy
CYOJ Hit Lab 13U Academy
Payment Terms

Please review this policy carefully before completing the initial team payment.

Section 01

Full-Season Financial Commitment

The total 2027 CYOJ Hit Lab 13U Baseball Academy team-package amount is $2,500 per athlete.

Important Accepting the roster spot is a commitment to the full $2,500 season package.

Once the athlete's Academy roster spot has been accepted and the initial required payment has been successfully completed, the family is financially responsible for the full $2,500 team-package amount.

The financial commitment applies whether the family chooses to pay the entire amount at once or selects the available installment plan.

Section 02

Payment Options

Families may choose either the pay-in-full option or the installment option displayed on the 13U Academy payment page.

Pay in full One secure payment $2,500
Installment plan — Initial payment Due at initial Stripe checkout $1,000
Second installment November 1, 2026 $750
Final installment February 1, 2027 $750

Section 03

Installment Authorization

When the installment plan is selected, the payer authorizes the initial $1,000 payment and the remaining scheduled installments shown in this policy and during checkout.

The payment method used through Stripe may be securely saved for the remaining scheduled installments. CYOJ Hit Lab does not directly store the payer's complete card or bank-account credentials.

The scheduled installment amounts are:

  • $750 scheduled for November 1, 2026.
  • $750 scheduled for February 1, 2027.

A later scheduled installment is not intended to be processed until the prior required payment has been confirmed as paid.

Section 04

Voluntary Withdrawal

If an athlete voluntarily withdraws, quits or otherwise chooses not to continue with the 13U Baseball Academy after the roster spot has been accepted and the initial required payment has been successfully completed, the withdrawal does not cancel the family's remaining financial obligation.

Remaining balance

Voluntary withdrawal does not cancel scheduled installments or release the family from the unpaid portion of the $2,500 season-package commitment.

This policy applies because Academy expenses and commitments may be incurred based on accepted roster spots, including tournament planning, team insurance, uniforms and gear, training resources, facility access and other season-related program costs.

Section 05

Non-Refundable Payments

Payments already made toward the 2027 13U Baseball Academy team package are non-refundable following acceptance of the roster spot and successful completion of the initial required payment, except where CYOJ Hit Lab approves an exception in writing or where otherwise required by applicable law.

Missing practices, games, tournaments, training opportunities, clinics or other scheduled activities due to an athlete's personal schedule, voluntary absence, family decision, transportation issue or voluntary withdrawal does not automatically create a right to a refund, credit or reduction of the remaining team-package balance.

Section 06

Extraordinary Circumstances

A family experiencing extraordinary circumstances may submit a written request to CYOJ Hit Lab asking that the remaining financial obligation or a prior payment be reviewed.

Submission of a request does not guarantee approval. Any exception, credit, refund, payment adjustment or release from a remaining balance must be specifically approved by CYOJ Hit Lab in writing.

CYOJ Hit Lab may evaluate such requests individually based on the circumstances, timing, costs already committed or incurred, and other relevant program considerations.

No automatic exception

Medical issues, relocation, schedule conflicts, participation in another team or program, changes in personal preference, or other circumstances do not automatically cancel the family's full-season financial commitment.

Section 07

Program & Schedule Changes

Tournament selections, practice schedules, training locations, event dates and other Academy programming may change during the season because of weather, facility availability, field availability, tournament availability, scheduling needs, player safety or other operational considerations.

Reasonable program or scheduling changes do not automatically cancel the family's season-package commitment or create an automatic right to a refund.

This provision addresses ordinary operational and scheduling changes. Any circumstances involving cancellation of the Academy program itself will be addressed separately by CYOJ Hit Lab as appropriate and consistent with applicable law.

Section 08

Written Withdrawal Notice

Families who decide that an athlete will no longer participate in the 13U Baseball Academy should provide written notice to CYOJ Hit Lab as soon as reasonably possible.

Written withdrawal notice should be sent to:

academyteams@cyojhitlab.com

Providing withdrawal notice allows the Academy to update team planning and communications. Withdrawal notice itself does not cancel or reduce the remaining financial obligation unless CYOJ Hit Lab separately approves an adjustment in writing.

Section 09

Relationship to Other Academy Terms

This Payment & Cancellation Policy should be reviewed together with the 2027 13U Baseball Academy Agreement, CYOJ Hit Lab facility rules, applicable privacy practices and the current CYOJ Hit Lab Athlete Waiver.

The payment terms selected and authorized during Stripe checkout are also part of the payer's financial authorization.
